01 / Her Story

From a Love of Movement to a World of Possibility

"Hard work and dedication can take you to the global stage, regardless of where your journey began."

What started as a love for movement in primary school has blossomed into an inspiring journey of passion, dedication and exceptional achievement.

Raaniyaa has loved dancing from a very young age. Whether volunteering for school concerts, practising for hours or studying dance videos, she dedicates almost all of her free time outside schoolwork to her passion.

To stay active and grow, she began attending after-school MOD dance classes and was later introduced to a non-profit dance club for competitive training.

Her commitment, sacrifice and hours of hard work have yielded incredible results. Since last year, Raaniyaa has placed on the podium in 90% of her competitions.

More Than a Competition. A Chance to Grow.

This international exposure will allow Raaniyaa to compete with talented dancers from around the world, learn new techniques, build confidence and grow as an individual.

By representing South Africa, she hopes to inspire other young dancers in her community and show what is possible through hard work and dedication.
